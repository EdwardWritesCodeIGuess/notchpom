INSTALL NOTCHPOM

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later.
No developer tools or GitHub account needed.

1. Drag NotchPom into the Applications shortcut in this disk image.
2. Eject the NotchPom disk image.
3. Open Applications and double-click NotchPom.
4. Click the countdown in your menu bar, set Focus and Break minutes,
   and click Start. There is no Dock icon.

FIRST-LAUNCH MESSAGE FROM MACOS?

This community test build is ad-hoc signed, without Apple notarization.
If macOS says the developer cannot be verified or Apple could not verify
the app is free of malware:

1. Try opening NotchPom once, then dismiss the message.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll to Security and click Open Anyway for NotchPom.
4. Confirm Open and authenticate if asked.

Only use this exception for a copy you intentionally downloaded from:
https://github.com/EdwardWritesCodeIGuess/notchpom/releases

If your Mac is managed by school or work, your administrator may need
to allow it. Do not disable Gatekeeper. More from Apple:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

UPDATING

Quit NotchPom from its menu bar controls, then replace the old app in
Applications with the new version. Your duration preferences stay saved.

USING IT

- Pause before editing Focus or Break (1-180 minutes).
- Changing a duration resets the current session.
- Click the small timer beside the notch to start or pause.
- On a Mac without a notch, it appears below the center of the menu bar.
- The next session waits for you unless auto-start is enabled.
- Quit from the controls. To uninstall, quit and move the app to Trash.

Liquid Glass is available on macOS 26+. Earlier versions use frosted
material. Appearance follows your Mac's accessibility settings.
